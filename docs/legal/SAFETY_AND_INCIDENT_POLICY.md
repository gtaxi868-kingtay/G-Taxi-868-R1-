## 1. IN AN EMERGENCY, CALL 999 FIRST

If someone is in immediate danger, **call 999 (police) or 811 (ambulance)
before you contact us**. We are a technology platform. We are not an emergency
service and we cannot dispatch one.

The app's SOS button is there to raise the alarm with the people who can help you
— it is not a substitute for calling.

---

## 2. WHAT THE SOS BUTTON DOES

There is an **SOS** control on the active-ride screen. When you use it:

1. The alert is **recorded** with the trip, both parties, and the last known
   location.
2. Your saved **emergency contact** is messaged with your name, your driver's
   name and phone number, and the vehicle's last known location.
3. You receive a confirmation on your phone.
4. If the alert cannot be sent for any reason, the app offers to **dial emergency
   services directly**.

**Add an emergency contact before you need one.** Without one, step 2 cannot
happen. It is in your profile settings and takes a minute.

Both riders and drivers can trigger it. Only the two people on that trip can
trigger it for that trip — nobody else can raise an alert on your ride.

## 3. BEFORE AND DURING A TRIP

### For clients
- **Check the vehicle before you get in.** The app shows the plate, make, model
  and driver's photo. If any of it does not match, do not get in. Cancel — you
  will not be charged (see the
  [Refund and Cancellation Policy](REFUND_AND_CANCELLATION_POLICY.md), 2.4).
- Wait indoors or somewhere well-lit until the driver is close.
- Sit in the back where you can.
- Share your trip with someone if you are travelling at night.
- Wear the seatbelt.

### For operators
- Check the client's name against the app before starting.
- Do not start a trip you have not accepted in the app — an off-platform trip has
  no record, no support, and no evidence if something goes wrong.
- Do not drive tired, or after drinking.
- Do not use a handheld phone while driving.
- If you feel unsafe, end the trip somewhere public and report it.

## 4. WHAT WE CHECK BEFORE SOMEONE DRIVES

Every operator must upload, and keep current:
- A valid driver's permit
- Motor vehicle insurance meeting the **Motor Vehicles Insurance (Third-Party
  Risks) Act Chap. 48:51**
- Valid vehicle registration
- Any licence or badge required for carrying passengers or goods for reward

A person reviews each document and records the decision. **We track expiry dates
and restrict the account when a document lapses.**

**What this is not:** we are not a licensing authority. Holding the correct
authorisation to carry passengers for reward is the operator's own legal
responsibility, and our recording a document does not grant it. See section 3 of
the [Driver and Operator Agreement](DRIVER_OPERATOR_AGREEMENT.md).

## 5. WHAT IS NOT ALLOWED

Any of the following ends an account, for a client, an operator or a merchant:

- Violence, or the threat of it
- Sexual harassment or assault
- Discrimination on race, sex, religion, disability, or any other protected
  ground
- Driving under the influence
- Carrying a weapon on a trip
- Theft, including keeping something left in a vehicle
- Recording someone without their knowledge
- Contacting someone after a trip for anything other than a lost item
- Bringing anyone onto a trip who is not part of the booking

Serious matters are reported to the police. We do not treat this as an internal
matter when it is a crime.

## 6. REPORTING SOMETHING

| How | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **999 / 811** | Anything happening now |
| SOS in the app | Danger during a trip |
| App → the trip → **Get help** | Anything after the fact |
| support@gtaxi.tt | Anything, with detail and photographs |
| WhatsApp +1 868 703 1000 | Anything |

Report as soon as you can. We hold the trip's location trace for 90 days and the
full trip record for longer — the sooner you tell us, the more evidence exists.

Give us: the trip or order number, what happened, when, and anything you have —
photographs, screenshots, a police report number.

---

## 7. WHAT WE DO WITH A REPORT

1. **Acknowledge** it.
2. **Suspend** the account complained about, straight away, where the report
   involves violence, a sexual offence, a weapon, or driving under the influence.
   We do this before investigating, not after.
3. **Investigate** — the trip record, the location trace, both accounts' history,
   and both accounts' side of it.
4. **Decide** — no action, warning, retraining, suspension, or permanent removal.
5. **Tell you the outcome**, in general terms. We cannot tell you what we did to
   another person's account; that is their personal information.

**We will not pair you with that person again**, whatever the outcome.

You are never penalised for making a report in good faith.

## 8. YOUR PRIVACY DURING AN INVESTIGATION

- We use the trip record, the location trace, and what both of you tell us.
- We share what a police investigation lawfully requires.
- We do not give your contact details to the other party.
- Investigation records are held while the matter is open, then under the
  schedule in the
  [Data Retention and Deletion Notice](DATA_RETENTION_AND_DELETION.md).

## 9. WHAT IS BUILT, AND WHAT IS NOT — HONEST STATEMENT

We would rather be plain about this than imply a safety net that is not there.

**Built and working today:**
- The SOS button on the active-ride screen, for both riders and drivers.
- Alert recorded against the trip, with the last known location.
- Emergency contact messaged automatically, where one is saved.
- Fallback to dialling emergency services if the alert cannot send.
- Only the two people on a trip can raise an alert for it — verified server-side.
- Driver document review, with expiry tracking and account restriction.
- Location trace retained for 90 days, so an investigation has evidence.

**Not built yet — and it matters:**
- **An SOS alert does not currently page anyone at G-Taxi in real time.** It is
  recorded and your emergency contact is messaged, but no staff member is woken
  up by it. The confirmation message says a safety specialist will review the
  alert; **today that review happens when someone next opens the dashboard, not
  immediately.**
- There is no 24/7 staffed safety line. Support is email and WhatsApp, answered
  during working hours.
- Automatic detection of a long stop or an off-route trip is not built.

**Because of this, section 1 is not a formality.** Until real-time alerting and a
staffed line exist, **calling 999 is the fastest route to help, not the backup
one.** This policy should not be published with any response-time promise the
operator cannot actually staff.

## 10. INSURANCE

Operators carry their own insurance (section 3 of the
[Driver and Operator Agreement](DRIVER_OPERATOR_AGREEMENT.md)).

**G-Taxi does not provide accident, injury or liability cover** for clients,
operators or merchants. A claim arising from a trip is made against the
operator's insurance.

## 11. LOST PROPERTY

Report it through the trip in the app within 24 hours. We will pass a message to
the operator. Returning an item is arranged between the two of you, and the
operator may reasonably ask for the cost of returning it.

Keeping something a client left behind is theft, and it ends an account.
