## 2. WHAT WE DO AND DO NOT DO

**We provide a platform.** Clients find you through it, place orders, and pay
through it. We arrange an independent operator to collect and deliver where the
order requires it, and we record and settle the money.

**We do not:**
- Buy your stock or take ownership of it
- Set your prices — you do
- Employ you or your staff
- Employ the operators who collect from you (see the
  [Driver and Operator Agreement](DRIVER_OPERATOR_AGREEMENT.md))

You remain the seller of everything you list. Your obligations to the client
under the **Consumer Protection and Safety Act, 2020 (Act 13 of 2020)** are
yours.

## 4. MONEY

### 4.1 Commission
> *The rate below is the platform's configured default. The operator must confirm
> the final rate and any per-category variation before this is published.*

G-Taxi retains a commission on the value of goods sold through the platform. Your
current rate is shown in your merchant dashboard and is the rate that applies —
this document does not override it.

Delivery fees are separate from your goods revenue and are split between the
operator and the platform. They do not reduce your share of the goods.

### 4.2 Node fees
Where you host a verified kiosk node and a job originates there, you earn a node
fee equal to **2% of the platform's own share** of that job. This is paid from
the platform's share, not from the operator's earnings.

A node fee is only paid where the origin is verified — either by a genuine NFC
tap at your node within the last 30 minutes, or by the client being physically
within your node's geofence. **A claimed origin without verification earns
nothing.** This protects honest hosts from having their commission redirected.

### 4.3 Rider discounts
You may choose to offer a client discount from your own margin. This is
opt-in, set by you, and can be switched off at any time. We will never apply a
discount that comes out of your margin without your having enabled it.

### 4.4 Settlement
Order proceeds, less commission, are credited to your merchant wallet when the
order completes. Payout follows the schedule shown in your dashboard.

Where you accept cash directly, the platform's commission becomes a balance owed
by you and is settled against future proceeds or paid through the app.

### 4.5 Deductions
We may deduct from your wallet:
- Commission on cash orders you collected
- Refunds to clients caused by your fault — missing items, wrong items,
  unacceptable condition
- Chargebacks arising from your orders
- Amounts obtained by fraud

Every deduction is itemised in your dashboard. We will not deduct anything not
listed here without telling you first.

### 4.6 Taxes
You are responsible for your own **VAT, income tax and all other statutory
obligations**, including issuing any receipt or invoice the law requires. We do
not collect or remit tax on your behalf.

## 5. ORDERS

- **Accept or decline promptly.** A client waiting on an unanswered order is the
  single worst experience on the platform.
- Keep your catalogue and availability current. Repeatedly selling items you do
  not have is grounds for suspension.
- Prepare orders within the time you indicate.
- Where an item is unavailable, propose a substitute through the app. **The
  client decides.** Never substitute silently.
- Package food so it survives the journey. Seal it.
- Hand over only to the assigned operator shown in the app.
