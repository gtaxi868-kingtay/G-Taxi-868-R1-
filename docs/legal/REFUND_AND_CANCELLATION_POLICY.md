## 1. THE SHORT VERSION

- Cancel a ride quickly and it costs you nothing.
- Cancel after a driver is already on the way and a small fee may apply, because
  that driver spent time and fuel getting to you.
- If the driver cancels, or nobody comes, **you pay nothing**.
- If you were charged for something you did not receive, tell us and we refund
  it.
- Nothing here removes the rights you have under the **Consumer Protection and
  Safety Act, 2020 (Act 13 of 2020)**.

## 2. RIDES

### 2.1 Cancelling before a driver accepts
Free. Always. No fee under any circumstances.

### 2.2 Cancelling after a driver accepts
> *Proposed, not yet enforced in software — the operator must confirm the window
> and amount before this is published.*

- Free within the first **2 minutes** after a driver accepts.
- After that, a cancellation fee of **TT$10** may apply, paid to the driver for
  their time and fuel.

### 2.3 Cancelling after the driver has arrived
> *Proposed, not yet enforced in software.*

A cancellation fee of **TT$15** may apply, paid to the driver.

### 2.4 When you are never charged
- The driver cancels.
- No driver accepts your request and it expires.
- The driver did not arrive within a reasonable time and you cancelled.
- The driver arrived in a vehicle that does not match the app.
- You cancelled for a safety reason.
- The app failed and the ride could not proceed.

### 2.5 Waiting time
A driver may wait a reasonable period at your pickup point. If you do not appear
and the driver cancels, the fee in 2.3 may apply. If you tell the driver you are
delayed and they agree to wait, no fee applies.

### 2.6 Disputed fares
If the fare charged does not match the trip taken — a route far longer than
needed, a trip that ended somewhere other than your destination, a duplicate
charge — contact us. We hold a complete record of the trip and the split, and we
will correct a genuine error.

## 3. FOOD, GROCERY AND MERCHANT DELIVERY

### 3.1 Cancelling an order
- **Before the merchant accepts:** free, full refund.
- **After the merchant accepts but before preparation begins:** free where the
  merchant confirms nothing has been made yet.
- **After preparation begins:** generally not refundable, because the food has
  been made. We will always ask the merchant first.
- **After collection by the driver:** not cancellable. Use 3.2 instead.

### 3.2 Something was wrong with the order
Tell us within **48 hours** of delivery. Photographs help. We refund where:
- Items are missing
- The wrong items arrived
- Food arrived in an unacceptable condition
- The order never arrived

We refund the affected items, or the whole order where most of it was wrong. The
delivery fee is refunded where the fault was in delivery.

### 3.3 Substitutions
Where a merchant is out of an item, they may propose a substitute. **You choose.**
If you reject it, that item is refunded. If nobody can reach you, the item is
removed and refunded.

### 3.4 Where the money comes from
A refund caused by the merchant is recovered from the merchant. A refund caused
by delivery is borne by the platform or the driver, depending on the cause. **This
never changes what you receive** — it only affects our internal settlement.

## 4. G-ESCAPE TRAVEL BOOKINGS — DIFFERENT RULES

**Read this carefully. Travel is not like a ride.**

When you book a flight-and-lodging package, we pay airlines and hotels on your
behalf. **Their cancellation rules apply to your booking, and we cannot override
them.**

### 4.1 Before your booking is confirmed
While a group package is still filling and has not been confirmed with the
airline or hotel, you may cancel and receive a **full refund**.

### 4.2 After confirmation
Once the airline and lodging are booked, refunds depend entirely on their terms.
Many airline fares in this region are **non-refundable**. We will tell you what
those terms are, for your specific booking, **before you pay**.

Where a refund is available, we pass on what the supplier returns, less any fee
they charge.

### 4.3 If we cancel
If a group package does not reach the numbers needed and we cancel it, you
receive a **full refund** including any platform fee.

### 4.4 Travel insurance
We strongly recommend it. We do not provide it.

## 7. HOW TO ASK FOR A REFUND

1. Open the app, find the trip or order, and tap **Get help**.
2. Or email **support@gtaxi.tt** with the trip or order number.
3. Or message **+1 868 703 1000** on WhatsApp.

**Tell us within 48 hours** for a food or delivery problem, and within **30 days**
for anything else. Earlier is better — we still hold the driver's location trace
and the full record.

### Timelines
| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| We acknowledge | 1 business day |
| We decide | 5 business days |
| Refund to wallet | Immediately on approval |
| Refund to card | 5–10 business days, set by your bank |
| Refund to cash payment | Credited to your wallet |

### If you disagree with our decision
Reply and ask for a review. A person, not an automated system, looks at it again.
If you are still not satisfied, you may take the matter to the **Consumer Affairs
Division** of the Ministry of Trade and Industry, or to court. This policy does
not remove that right.

## 8. FRAUD

We refuse refunds where a claim is not genuine, and we may close an account used
to make repeated false claims. We tell you when we do this and why. A refusal is
never automatic — a person reviews it.
