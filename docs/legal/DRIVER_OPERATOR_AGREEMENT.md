## 1. WHAT THIS AGREEMENT IS

This agreement is between you ("you", "the Operator") and G-Taxi Technology Ltd.
("G-Taxi", "we", "us"). It applies from the moment you create a driver or
operator account and continues until either side ends it.

By creating an account you confirm you have read this agreement and the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md), and that you accept both.

## 2. YOU ARE NOT OUR EMPLOYEE — READ THIS SECTION

This is the foundation of everything below.

**G-Taxi does not hire drivers. G-Taxi does not employ you.** We operate a
technology platform that lets a client ask someone to take them somewhere, bring
them food, or help them with a task. **The client pays you.** What the client
pays G-Taxi is a fee for making the connection.

In practice this means:

- You are an **independent contractor** running your own business.
- **You decide** when to go online, when to go offline, and which requests to
  accept or decline.
- There is no shift, no roster, no minimum hours, and no requirement to accept
  any particular job.
- We do not supply you with a vehicle, fuel, insurance or equipment. Those are
  yours.
- You are responsible for your own **income tax, National Insurance, and any
  other statutory contributions**. G-Taxi does not deduct or remit these on your
  behalf.
- You are free to work for any other platform or client at the same time,
  including a direct competitor.
- Nothing in this agreement creates employment, partnership, agency or a joint
  venture.

If a court or authority ever determines otherwise, the rest of this agreement
survives as far as the law allows.

A "commander" in this platform recruits merchants and operators into a territory.
**A commander does not hire you either.** A commander is another independent
business, and their earnings come from the platform's own share, never from
yours.

## 3. WHAT YOU MUST HAVE BEFORE YOU DRIVE

You must hold, keep current, and upload proof of:

- A valid **driver's permit** for the class of vehicle you use
- **Motor vehicle insurance** valid for the use you are making of the vehicle,
  meeting the **Motor Vehicles Insurance (Third-Party Risks) Act Chap. 48:51**
- Valid **vehicle registration**
- Any **licence, badge or plate** the law requires for carrying passengers or
  goods for reward, obtained from the Ministry of Works and Transport or its
  designated authority

**It is your responsibility to know and hold the correct authorisation for what
you are doing.** G-Taxi records the documents you supply and checks their expiry
dates. We do not, and cannot, grant you any legal right to operate.

If a document expires, we will restrict your account until you upload a current
one. If you supply a document that is false or altered, we will close your
account permanently and may report it.

## 4. HOW YOU GET PAID

### 4.1 The split
For each completed job, the amount the client pays is divided as follows, using
figures held in the platform's live pricing configuration:

| Share | Normal | Where the territory has an active commander |
|---|---|---|
| **You (the Operator)** | **80%** | **78%** |
| Platform fee | 15% | 15% |
| Platform reserve fund | 1.5% | 1.5% |
| Commander | — | 2% |
| Remainder (node/merchant attribution, adjustments) | balance | balance |

Where a job originates at a verified merchant node, a node fee of **2% of the
platform's own share** is paid to that merchant. **This never comes out of your
80%.**

Referral rewards paid to operators or clients come from the platform reserve
fund and, by design, cannot reduce your share.

### 4.2 Cash jobs
Where the client pays cash, you receive the fare directly. The platform's share
becomes a balance owed by you, settled against your future earnings or paid
through the app.

### 4.3 Wallet and card jobs
Your share is credited to your in-app wallet when the job completes. You may
withdraw it in accordance with the payout schedule shown in the app.

### 4.4 Surge
Fares may rise at busy times. Any surge is **capped at 2.0× the base fare** by a
hard limit in the system, not by policy alone. Surge is applied to the whole fare
and split by the same percentages above, so a surge raises your earnings too.

### 4.5 What we may deduct
We may deduct from your wallet:
- The platform's share of cash jobs you have collected
- Amounts you owe under a vehicle financing or lease arrangement you separately
  agreed to
- Refunds or chargebacks arising from a job where you were at fault
- Amounts obtained by fraud

We will show you a record of every deduction. We will not deduct anything not
listed here without telling you first.

### 4.6 Errors
If we underpay you, tell us and we will correct it. If we overpay you, we may
recover the amount from future earnings, and we will tell you before we do.

## 5. WHAT WE ASK OF YOU

- Carry out jobs you accept, safely and lawfully.
- Do not drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Do not use a handheld phone while driving.
- Keep the vehicle roadworthy and reasonably clean.
- Treat clients, merchants and other operators with respect. Harassment,
  discrimination, threats and violence are grounds for immediate removal.
- Do not carry anyone other than the client and their party on a job.
- Do not ask a client to cancel and pay you off-platform for a job the platform
  arranged. This is the one thing that directly takes money from the service you
  are relying on.
- Do not share a client's details with anyone.
- Keep your account to yourself. Do not let another person drive on your account.

## 6. RATINGS AND ACCOUNT STANDING

Clients rate jobs. Ratings are shown to you and are used to identify safety
problems, not to set quotas.

We may **suspend** your account while we investigate a safety complaint, a fraud
signal, or an expired document. We will tell you why, in the app.

We may **end** your account for:
- A serious safety incident
- Fraud, including false documents or manufactured jobs
- Repeated, substantiated complaints
- Loss of a licence, permit or insurance required in section 3

If your account ends, **any earnings you have already made remain yours** and
will be paid out, less anything properly owed under 4.5.

## 7. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY

**You must carry your own insurance.** G-Taxi does not insure you, your vehicle,
your passengers or your cargo, and does not provide occupational injury cover.

You are responsible for what happens during a job. G-Taxi's role is limited to
connecting you with the client and handling the payment record.

Our total liability to you, for any claim connected to this agreement, is limited
to the platform fees we retained from your jobs in the **three months** before
the claim arose. Nothing in this section limits liability that cannot lawfully be
limited, including for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or for
fraud.

## 8. YOUR DATA

We handle your personal information as set out in the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md). Points that matter most to you:

- Your **live location is shared only while you are online**, and only with the
  client of an active job and with our operations team.
- Your identity documents are held in **private** storage. Links to them are
  time-limited.
- A client sees your first name, photo, vehicle and plate. They do not see your
  address, your email, or your other jobs.
- We keep your documents and earnings records after your account closes, because
  regulators and tax law require it.

## 9. ENDING THIS AGREEMENT

**You** may stop at any time, for any reason. Go offline and, if you wish, delete
your account in the app. There is no notice period and no penalty.

**We** may end it on 14 days' notice for any reason, or immediately for anything
in section 6.

On ending: withdraw your wallet balance, and settle anything owed under 4.5.
Sections 2, 7, 8 and 11 survive.

If we change this agreement in a way that affects your pay or your obligations,

accept the new version. Your acceptance of each version is recorded. If you do
not accept, you may stop using the platform; we will not change your terms
retroactively for jobs already completed.
