1. Who we are

G-Taxi is a platform in Trinidad and Tobago. It connects riders with independent drivers, lets people order from local merchants for delivery, sells G-Escape group travel packages, and runs a network of “G nodes”: NFC tap points and QR codes at partner locations, set up by area leads called G-Leads.

2. Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use G-Taxi. Most of it comes from you. Some is created as you use the service, such as ride records and location, and some comes from other users, such as a rating a driver gives you.

2.1 Visiting the website

When you load gtaxi.tt, your browser connects to our website host (Vercel) and to Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces. On the home page it also connects to jsDelivr, which supplies an animation library. Each of these receives your IP address and standard browser information, such as browser type and the page requested.

The home page also contacts our backend (Supabase) to load our support WhatsApp number. If you arrived by scanning a G-Taxi QR code or tapping an NFC tag, the tag or location code in the link is sent too, so we can show you the right place. Website analytics are covered in section 8.

2.2 Joining the waitlist

The waitlist form asks for your name, WhatsApp number, community and the role you are joining as (Rider, Driver, G-Partner or G-Lead). We also record that the sign-up came from the website.

When our team follows up, we may add when you were contacted and by whom, whether you were approved, which G-Lead covers your area, and any details you give us, such as your vehicle for driving or your business type for a store.

2.3 Creating an account

  • Name, email address, phone number and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not visible to us in plain text.
  • Your role (rider, driver, merchant, G-Lead or administrator), and a profile photo if you add one.
  • A record that you accepted the Terms and this Privacy Policy, with the date and time.
  • Any referral code or G-Lead code you enter when signing up, and who referred you.
  • Your device’s push notification token and your notification settings.

2.4 Riders

  • Pickup, drop-off and stop addresses and map coordinates, plus scheduled ride times.
  • Ride history: route, distance, duration, vehicle class, fare, waiting charges, payment method, tips and cancellations.
  • Saved places (for example “Home”) with their address and coordinates.
  • An emergency contact’s name and phone number, if you add one.
  • A selfie, if the app asks you to verify yourself before a ride. The photo is stored in a private storage area, and your phone remembers that the device has been verified.
  • Grocery and merchant orders, delivery addresses, cart contents and appointment bookings.
  • Photos you take with the product scanner or the photo-based pickup finder (see 2.13).
  • Ride and assistant preferences (for example a quiet ride, routes to avoid, AI settings), drivers you prefer, people you block, and members of family or trusted-contact groups you set up.
  • Your progress and rewards, frequent places, and the merchants you use. These are built from your activity and used for suggestions and rewards.

2.5 Drivers

  • Name, phone number, vehicle model, plate number, vehicle class and registration class.
  • Photos of the front and back of your driving licence and of your vehicle.
  • Compliance documents you submit (insurance, licence, vehicle registration, PSV badge), with expiry dates and the outcome of our review.
  • Emergency contact details, and bank account details for payouts.
  • Earnings, payouts, platform fees, amounts owed, acceptance rate and ratings.
  • Online status and location, including a trail of location points (see 2.10), plus records of location readings our systems flag as implausible.
  • Photos of receipts you upload in the wallet.
  • If you apply for a vehicle lease or savings plan: your signed consent to earnings deductions (with version and date), an earnings summary (number of rides, total and average earnings) sent as proof of income, and your savings balance. If you ask about buying a vehicle, we also keep that request.

2.6 Merchants

  • Business name, login email and password.
  • Business address, map location, category, opening hours, delivery settings and photos.
  • Staff accounts linked to your store.
  • Payout bank details (bank, account holder, account number, account type), if you provide them.
  • Orders, commissions, fees, billing and any rider discount you choose to offer.
  • For rides you book on a customer’s behalf, the customer’s phone number and any name or note you enter.

2.7 G-Leads

Phone and WhatsApp numbers, the area you are applying for, application and review status, the reference for your application payment, your territory, the drivers and merchants you recruit, revenue share, and payout details.

2.8 G-Escape travel

  • Booking details: package, number of guests, price, payment method and references, airport-transfer pickup address and coordinates, special requests and booking reference.
  • Travel document details you submit for the airline booking: full name as on your passport, date of birth, gender, nationality, passport number, issue date, expiry date and place of issue, place of birth, email, phone, emergency contact and dietary notes.
  • Passenger names and a passport image, where these are added to a booking.
  • Routes you have registered interest in.

2.9 Payments, wallet and cash codes

  • Cards through Stripe: you enter card details into Stripe’s payment form, not ours. We keep a Stripe customer ID and payment references. When you pay by card, Stripe saves the card to your Stripe customer record so it can be used for later rides.
  • Cards through WiPay: you enter card details on WiPay’s payment page. We keep the order and transaction references.
  • We do not store card numbers or security codes anywhere in our systems.
  • Wallet: every credit and debit, including top-ups, fares, tips, refunds, cancellation fees, rewards and withdrawals.
  • Cash withdrawal codes: the amount, fee, code, the driver who redeems it, and when it was created, redeemed or expired.
  • Split payments: who joined a split and each person’s share.
  • Withdrawal requests: amount, fee, the phone number given, and bank reference.

2.10 Location

  • Riders: with your permission, the Rider app uses your precise location while the app is open, to show where you are, set your pickup and show your trip on the map. It does not ask for background location. What we store is the pickup, drop-off and stop locations on each ride, plus your coordinates if you use the photo-based pickup finder.
  • Drivers: while you are online, the Driver app sends your precise location, heading and speed, including when the app is in the background. We store your current position and a trail of location points. We use them for dispatch, fares, safety monitoring, dispute resolution and detecting falsified locations.
  • Merchants: your store’s location.
  • Website visitors: we do not request your location. Your IP address may indicate roughly where you are.

2.11 Safety and SOS

If a rider presses SOS during a ride, we create an incident record. It holds the ride, rider and driver, the time, the rider’s and driver’s names, the driver’s phone number and the driver’s location.

We send a WhatsApp message to the rider’s emergency contact, if one is saved. It includes the rider’s name, the driver’s name and phone number, and the location. If no contact is saved, we send the rider a confirmation instead.

Our systems also run automatic safety checks on trips and deliveries. These record safety events linked to an area, and at first to the driver, ride or order involved. We also keep reports that users file about each other.

2.12 Messages, ratings and support

  • In-trip chat messages between rider and driver.
  • Ratings and optional comments.
  • Support requests and disputes: subject, description, category, the ride involved, our notes, and any refund decision.
  • WhatsApp messages and push notifications we send you, and their delivery receipts.

2.13 AI features and voice commands

  • Voice and typed commands on the Rider home screen: we receive the words as text. The app does not upload audio recordings.
  • Chat with G (the rider assistant): the messages you send, up to 1,000 characters each. Suggestions are on by default and can be turned off. G’s memory is off by default. If you switch it on, G saves short notes about your preferences, and it can set reminders you ask for.
  • Photos you send to identify a product, or to work out your pickup spot from your surroundings.
  • Personalised greetings built from your first name and ride patterns.

2.14 NFC taps and QR codes

When you tap a G node, a G-Taxi tag or an event wristband, or scan a G-Taxi QR code, we record the tag ID, the location or merchant it belongs to, your account (if you are signed in), the type of event, the time and the result.

Tags and wristbands can be linked to your profile. We also use taps to check that a ride really started at a partner location before crediting that merchant.

2.15 Device and technical information

This includes push notification tokens, and browser or device details recorded when you accept a policy. It also includes records used to limit repeated requests, error reports, and server logs kept by our hosting providers. For administrator accounts, we keep an audit log of actions, including IP address.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the service: matching riders with drivers, dispatching deliveries, running G-Escape bookings, processing payments and keeping your wallet.
  • To calculate fares and pay people: splitting each payment between the driver, the platform, a capital reserve, and where applicable the G-Lead and partner merchant.
  • To verify people: reviewing driver documents and approving merchants, merchant locations and G-Lead applications.
  • For safety: SOS alerts, trip safety checks, identity checks before rides, and investigating incidents.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse: detecting falsified locations, repeated cancellations, false merchant attribution and misuse of referral rewards.
  • For support and disputes: answering requests, reviewing fares and deciding refunds.
  • To communicate: ride updates, receipts, booking confirmations, reminders and personalised daily notifications, sent by push notification or WhatsApp.
  • To personalise: saved places, suggested stops and merchants, greetings and rewards.
  • To plan and improve: demand forecasts, deciding when to open a community, and internal business reporting.
  • To meet legal duties: keeping financial records and responding to lawful requests.

4. AI and automated processing

Rider assistant and voice commands. Your message or command is sent to an outside AI service, together with context such as the names and addresses of your saved places, merchants you have used recently, and your first name. G’s answers can be wrong, so check a suggestion before relying on it.

G, the operations assistant. G helps our team by reading business records and proposing actions. Those records include ride and payment totals, open support requests and their contents, and driver compliance details such as names and plate numbers. Anything G proposes goes to an approval inbox. It does nothing until an administrator approves it, and approved actions run through fixed, reviewed code, not through the AI. Nothing G proposes that involves money can happen without that human approval.

Photos. Product photos and pickup-spot photos are sent to Google’s Gemini service. Driver receipt photos are sent to Google Cloud Vision to read the text.

Automated rules that affect you. Some decisions are made automatically by fixed rules, not AI:

  • Prices can rise in busy areas.
  • Riders may be charged a cancellation fee in the situations described in the Terms.
  • A driver’s acceptance rate drops when they cancel a ride they accepted.
  • A driver account can be suspended pending review if our systems detect falsified location readings.

If you think an automated decision about you is wrong, contact support and a person will review it.

5. Who else can see your information

  • Riders and drivers on a trip. During an active ride, each can see the other’s name and the information needed to complete the trip: pickup and drop-off, vehicle and plate, live driver location, and in-app chat. A driver can see the assigned rider’s profile only while the ride is in progress.
  • Merchants see the orders placed with them and the delivery details needed to fulfil them.
  • G-Leads see the drivers and merchants in their territory and the performance and earnings information behind their revenue share.
  • Your emergency contact receives the SOS alert described in 2.11.
  • Customers of merchant-booked rides receive a WhatsApp message with trip tracking details.
  • Other people in a split payment see the split and each share.
  • Vehicle financing partners and car dealers receive your lease application, earnings summary or vehicle purchase request, if you apply or ask.
  • Airlines, accommodation and transfer providers receive the traveller details needed to complete a G-Escape booking.
  • Our staff can access information when needed to run and support the service. Administrator actions are logged.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or when needed to protect someone’s safety or investigate fraud.

We do not sell personal information.

7. Data stored outside Trinidad and Tobago

Our database and file storage are hosted by Supabase in the United States. Most of the providers in section 6 process information outside Trinidad and Tobago, mainly in the United States. By using G-Taxi, your information will be transferred to and stored in those countries, where data protection laws may differ from those of Trinidad and Tobago.

8. Cookies and website storage

The gtaxi.tt website does not set cookies or store information in your browser's local storage for tracking unless you accept analytics below. We do store your accept/decline choice itself in local storage, so we do not ask on every visit.

Analytics only with your permission. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It loads only after you accept analytics in the cookie banner, and it sets cookies in your browser once it does. If you decline, or ignore the banner, nothing loads and no analytics cookie is set. Your choice is remembered on your device, and you can change it at any time.

Fonts and libraries. Whatever you choose, loading the site sends your IP address to Google Fonts and, on the home page, jsDelivr, as described in 2.1.

In the apps. The mobile apps keep your sign-in session in your phone’s secure storage. The Rider app also stores small settings on the device, such as whether the device has been verified.

10. Security

We protect information with access controls in the database, so each user can reach only their own records and what they need for an active trip. Money-moving operations and administrator functions run on the server, where every request is checked against the signed-in account. Identity documents, verification photos and receipts are kept in private storage.

Card details are handled by Stripe and WiPay, not by us. Administrator actions are logged. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused.

11. Your rights and choices

About the law

Trinidad and Tobago’s Data Protection Act, 2011 has only been partly brought into force. Some of its provisions, including those that would give individuals enforceable rights against private companies through the Information Commissioner, have not yet been proclaimed. Whatever the legal position, we offer the choices below and follow the Act’s general privacy principles.

Access and correction

You can see and edit most of your profile in the app. To get a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or to correct something you cannot change yourself, email [[CONTACT EMAIL]] from the address on your account. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on the request.

Deleting your account

You can ask to delete your account from the profile screen in the app, or by emailing [[CONTACT EMAIL]]. After you ask, there is a 30-day window during which you can change your mind. After that, we remove the information that identifies you:

  • Your name, email, phone number, profile photo, push token, emergency contact, hashed ID number and linked NFC tag, all removed or replaced with a placeholder.
  • For drivers: name, phone number, emergency contact, bank details on the driver profile, and location.
  • Saved places, G’s memory and reminders, G-Escape travel document details, pickup and drop-off addresses on your rides, and chat messages you sent.
  • Files you uploaded to profile photos, receipts, verification photos and merchant intake photos, which are queued for deletion.

What we keep. We keep records we need for legal and financial reasons, such as fares, wallet transactions and booking records, with your identity removed. Deletion is paused while a safety incident or an unresolved support request involving you is still open.

Your wallet. Withdraw any wallet balance before the 30-day window ends. It cannot be returned afterwards.

Not yet covered automatically. Driver licence and vehicle photos, compliance documents, payout bank account records held separately from the driver profile, merchant payout details, and waitlist entries are not yet removed by this process. Tell us if you want them removed and we will review the request, subject to records we must keep.

Other choices

  • AI: turn G’s suggestions or memory off in the app, or use “forget” to delete G’s saved memory and pending reminders straight away.
  • Notifications: change notification settings in the app or in your phone’s settings.
  • Location: control location permission in your phone’s settings. Rides cannot be booked, and drivers cannot go online, without it.
  • Website analytics: decline or withdraw consent in the cookie banner.
  • Waitlist: email [[CONTACT EMAIL]] to be removed.
