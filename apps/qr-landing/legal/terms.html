1. About G-Taxi

G-Taxi is a technology platform in Trinidad and Tobago. It connects:

  • riders with drivers who provide transport and delivery services;
  • customers with local merchants;
  • travellers with G-Escape group travel packages.

G-Taxi also runs a network of partner locations (“G nodes”), set up and grown by area leads called G-Leads.

Drivers are independent operators, not G-Taxi employees (see section 7). Merchants are responsible for the goods and services they sell. Flights and accommodation in G-Escape packages are provided by airlines and accommodation providers.

3. Rides

3.1 Booking

You request a ride in the Rider app by choosing a pickup, a destination, any stops and a vehicle class. Before you confirm, the app shows an estimated fare. Rides can be requested now or scheduled for later. Heavier vehicle classes, such as trucks, are available only when we switch them on.

3.2 How fares are calculated

A fare is made up of:

  • a base fare;
  • a per-kilometre charge for the route distance;
  • a per-minute charge for trip time;
  • stop fees for any stops you add, which differ by type of stop (for example grocery or pharmacy).

That total is multiplied by the vehicle class rate (for example Standard, XL or Premium) and by any surge multiplier in effect. A minimum fare applies, and some vehicle classes have their own higher minimum. Current rates are shown in the app before you book, and we may change them.

3.3 Waiting charges

  • At pickup: the driver waits free for 3 minutes after arriving. After that, a per-minute waiting charge is added.
  • At merchant stops: a per-minute waiting charge applies while the driver waits at a partner merchant stop, with no free period.

Because waiting charges and changes to your route or stops are only known during the trip, your final fare can differ from the estimate.

3.4 Surge pricing

When demand is high in an area, prices there can rise, either because we set a surge area or automatically based on demand. Any surge in effect is included in the estimate shown before you book.

3.5 Cancellations

  • Riders can cancel free of charge while we are still looking for a driver.
  • Once a driver is assigned, a cancellation fee (currently TT$5) is charged if the driver has already arrived, or is less than 1 km from your pickup, when you cancel. The fee is taken from your wallet and paid to the driver.
  • A ride cannot be cancelled in the app once it has started.
  • Drivers who cancel a ride they have accepted see their acceptance rate go down. If a driver cancels after arriving, we check what happened next.
  • If your driver cancels, we do not automatically find you another driver. Please request a new ride.

3.6 Tips

After a ride you can tip your driver from your wallet balance. The whole tip goes to the driver. Tips are optional.

3.7 During the ride

You may be asked to confirm a ride PIN, or to take a verification selfie, before a ride starts. Treat your driver and their vehicle with respect. You are responsible for any damage you cause beyond normal use.

4. Payments and wallet

4.1 Ways to pay

  • Cash, paid to the driver, who confirms it in the Driver app.
  • Card, processed by Stripe or WiPay. Card payments are also subject to their terms. When you pay by card through Stripe, your card is saved with Stripe for later rides.
  • G-Taxi wallet, topped up in the app.
  • Split payment, where you share a fare with other people.
  • Tap to pay at supported G nodes.

You authorise us to charge the payment method you choose for fares, waiting charges, stop fees, cancellation fees and orders.

4.2 Wallet

Your wallet balance is the total of all your wallet transactions: top-ups, payments, refunds, tips, rewards and withdrawals. The balance can be spent only on G-Taxi and does not earn interest.

If you ask us to delete your account, withdraw your balance before the deletion is completed. A balance left in the wallet cannot be returned afterwards.

If you think a fare or charge is wrong, report it through support in the app. We review each report and may issue a refund or credit.

5. Deliveries and merchant orders

You can order from partner merchants for delivery, or add a merchant stop to a ride. The merchant is responsible for the items it sells: their quality, safety, description and price. A driver collects or shops for your order and delivers it. Delivery fees and any minimum order amount are shown before you order.

Merchants can also book a ride for their customers. If a merchant books one for you, you will get a WhatsApp message with trip details.

6. G-Escape travel packages

  • Seat holds. Reserving a package puts a hold on seats for a limited time. If a hold is not paid before it expires, the seats are released.
  • Group pricing. Some packages are priced by group size, and the price can drop as more people join before it is locked.
  • Payment and confirmation. You can pay with your wallet, by card or through WiPay. A booking is confirmed only when payment is taken and our team confirms it.
  • Travel documents. You must submit accurate passport and traveller details that match your travel documents. You are responsible for having a valid passport and any visas or entry documents you need.
  • Third-party providers. Flights and accommodation are provided by airlines and accommodation providers under their own terms. Airport transfer rides are provided through G-Taxi.
  • Changes and cancellations. If you need to change or cancel a G-Escape booking, contact us at gtaxi868@gmail.com and we will review your request.

7. Drivers

7.1 Independent operator

You provide services as an independent contractor, not as an employee, worker, partner or agent of G-Taxi. You decide when you go online, which requests you accept and how you run your service. You are responsible for your own taxes, insurance, licences and vehicle costs.

7.2 Requirements

  • A valid driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance, and where required a PSV badge. Upload them in the app and keep them current.
  • A vehicle that matches the class you registered and is registered for the type of service you provide.
  • We review documents before approving you, and may ask you to update them.

7.3 Location while online

While you are online, the Driver app collects your precise location, including in the background, for dispatch, fares and safety. Go offline when you are not available.

7.4 Earnings and fees

Each fare is split between you, the platform and a capital reserve. Current split:

  • Driver share: 80% of the fare.
  • G-Lead share: if your territory has an active G-Lead, 2% of the fare comes out of the driver share, so you receive 78%.
  • Capital reserve: 1.5% of the fare.
  • Platform: keeps the rest. Where a ride genuinely starts at a partner G node, a share of the platform’s portion (not yours) goes to that merchant.

Card and wallet earnings are credited to your G-Taxi wallet, and you can request a payout to your bank account. When you collect cash, the platform’s share becomes an amount you owe. Some features, such as redeeming cash codes, are unavailable until it is paid. We may change the split.

7.5 Acceptance, cancellations and location integrity

Cancelling rides you have accepted lowers your acceptance rate. Using apps or devices to fake your location is prohibited. If our systems detect falsified location readings, your account is suspended pending review.

7.6 Vehicle leasing and Earn-to-Deposit

If you apply for a vehicle lease, you sign a separate Earnings Assignment Addendum in the Driver app. It authorises deductions from your earnings and forms part of your agreement with us.

If you save toward a vehicle deposit through Earn-to-Deposit and leave the plan early, you get back a share of your savings based on how long you have been saving:

  • 100% within 30 days;
  • 75% within 90 days;
  • 50% within 180 days;
  • 25% after that.

The rest is kept by the platform.

10. G nodes, NFC tags and QR codes

Tapping a G node or scanning a G-Taxi QR code can open the app or website, start a ride from that location, or link a tag or wristband to your account. Tapping to request a ride costs you nothing extra.

Do not copy, move, damage or tamper with G-Taxi tags or QR codes, or use them to claim commission for rides that did not start there.

11. G, the AI assistant

G can answer questions, suggest destinations, stops and merchants, understand voice or typed commands, and set reminders. G’s answers are generated automatically and can be wrong. Check anything important, such as a destination, price or booking, before you rely on it.

On the operations side, G can propose actions to our team. Any action involving money must be approved by a human administrator before it takes effect.

12. Safety and SOS

The SOS button in a ride logs an incident for our team and sends an alert with your location to your saved emergency contact by WhatsApp. SOS is not an emergency service. If you are in danger, contact the police on 999 or other emergency services directly.

Add an emergency contact in the app, and only add someone who has agreed to receive these alerts. Report unsafe behaviour through the app.
